BYU-Idaho

Number of programs: BYU-Idaho offers over 100 bachelor's and associate degree options, focused on undergraduate teaching.

Admission Requierments: Applicants should have a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0. ACT/SAT scores are not required for admission; however, they are required for scholarship consideration. Applicants may also submit a test score if they feel it is a good representation of their academic abilities.

Location: 101 E Viking St, Rexburg, ID 83460, United States.


Ensign College

Number of programs: Ensign College offers 10 Bachelor of Applied Science (BAS) degrees, which have been redesigned into accelerated three-year programs.

Admission Requierments: Ensign College admissions require a high school diploma or equivalent, a $35 application fee, and an Ecclesiastical Endorsement.

Location: 95 N 300 W, Salt Lake City, UT 84101, Estados Unidos.


Eastern Arizona College

Number of programs: EAC offers over 90 academic programs to get on the fast-track to your dream career

Admission Requierments: a completed application, official high school transcripts or GED, and placement testing to enroll.

Location: 615 N Stadium Ave, Thatcher, AZ 85552, United States.
